## [2.0.6] - 2026-09-21

### Fixed

- **AutoDJ transitions**: mixing profiles apply their volume curves correctly again, without lowering the overall volume during the track change.
- **Manual skip in AutoDJ**: when you skip to a track that already has a prepared transition, KeyTune uses that plan even if the manual crossfade is turned off.
- **Audio output**: when you switch devices, playback preserves the position, volume and other states in use more reliably.

### Changed

- **Audio output selection**: `Alt+D` now opens a direct, accessible dialog to choose the playback device.
