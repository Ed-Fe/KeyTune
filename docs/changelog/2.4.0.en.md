## [2.4.0] - 2026-10-11

The main news in this version: KeyTune now follows your YouTube account from the inside. You read and reply to comments, receive notifications, join live chats, subscribe to channels, read posts and vote in polls. YouTube videos also get what music already had: history, resuming where you stopped (on your phone and TV too), radio and related videos, chapters and transcript.

YouTube features depend on how YouTube works on the inside. When it changes, something may stop working for no apparent reason. If that happens, update the additional resources or try again later.

### Added

#### YouTube account

- **Notifications**: shows the account's notification inbox. `Enter` plays the video and marks the notification as read. KeyTune checks the inbox every 15 minutes and speaks the new ones; with the program minimized, the notice goes to the Windows notification center. The interval and that notice are in **Preferences > KeyTube**.
- **Comments**: from the **Actions...** menu you can write, reply, like, mark **Dislike** and, on your own, edit and delete. **Sort by newest** swaps the top comments for the newest ones. What you write is published under your name.
- **Live chat**: **View the live chat**, in the **Actions...** menu of a broadcast on air, opens the chat in the list. New messages come in by themselves and are spoken. **Send a message...** is the last line of the list, and the list opens on it.
- **Channels**: **Channel subscription and notifications...** subscribes, unsubscribes and chooses what the channel notifies you of. **About the channel** opens the description and links, **Search in this channel...** lists the videos that match the text and **Posts** lists what the channel published. In a post, `Enter` reads the text, `Right arrow` opens the comments and a poll has **Vote in the poll...**.
- **Watch later and liked videos**: **Save to Watch later** and **Remove from Watch later**, in a video's **Actions...** menu. **Liked YouTube videos** lists what the account liked.
- **YouTube history**: the videos you watch go into the account's history, as YouTube Music tracks already did.

#### Playback

- **Resume where you stopped**: long videos come back from the point where you stopped, and **Continue listening** lists them. `Enter` on a video in **YouTube history** starts where the account left off. KeyTune tells YouTube how far you watched, so your phone and TV show the same point. It needs the account connected and YouTube.js.
- **Shorts**: opens a tab that plays one short at a time, repeating until you move on to the next one.
- **Chapters and transcript**: **View chapters** plays the video from the chosen point, and **View transcript** shows the subtitles as text, with the time of each line.

#### Home and lists

- **KeyTube home in groups**: **YouTube** and **YouTube Music** each hold what belongs to their service, while **Trending** and **Moods and genres** stay on their own. **Recommended on YouTube** and **For you on YouTube Music** are new.
- **Load the whole list**: in the tab's **Actions...** menu, brings what is missing from the list all at once. In a search or a channel, up to 1000 items come at a time.

#### Accessibility

- **Alert sounds**: short sounds tell you what happens in the chat, the account and the player without relying on speech, and play apart from the media volume. In **Preferences > Accessibility** you can turn them all off or one by one and replace an event's sound with your own audio.

### Changed

- **Radio and related videos for YouTube videos**: **Start radio from this track** (`Ctrl+R`) and related autoplay use the videos YouTube itself suggests. Before, they used the YouTube Music radio, which only made sense for songs.
- **Announcements that do not interrupt the screen reader**: announcements wait for the reader to finish speaking instead of cutting it off. In **Preferences > Accessibility** you can bring the old behavior back and choose which notices speak.
- **History in two lists**: **History** is now called **YouTube Music history**, next to the **YouTube history**. The option to save to the history now applies to videos too.
- **KeyTube actions menu**: the menu of the **Actions...** button (`Shift+F10`) shows only what applies to the selected item, in blocks. Before, it always had the same items, with the ones that did not apply disabled. **View contents** is now called **Open** on folders, channels and artists.
- **Items loaded at a time**: the **Playlists loaded at a time** option, in **Preferences > KeyTube**, was renamed and applies to every list of the tab. Before, searches, tracks and comments always came 20 at a time.
- **Long playlists load faster**: when going down a YouTube Music playlist, KeyTune reuses the tracks it had already fetched.
- **YouTube playlist without a limit**: opening or downloading a regular YouTube playlist brings every video. Before, it stopped at 200.

### Fixed

- **AutoDJ really off**: with AutoDJ turned off, a saved session no longer analyzes or mixes tracks, even with crossfade on. Analysis also stopped failing when Windows still held the temporary file.
- **KeyTune not reopening after closing or updating**: if shutdown hung, KeyTune stayed alive in the background with no window, and opening it again showed nothing. Shutdown now always finishes, and launching KeyTune ends a stalled copy. If an update does not finish, KeyTune says so the next time it opens.
- **Age-restricted videos**: with the YouTube account connected, videos that ask for age confirmation now play. Before, KeyTune only tried without the account and playback failed. These videos play with medium-quality video. Without the account, KeyTune explains that the video is age-restricted and what to do.
- **Complete liked songs**: the **Liked songs** list of YouTube Music shows every liked track, not just the first few hundred. It loads a part at a time: go down to the end to bring more.
- **YouTube titles in your language**: video lists of channels and playlists came with English titles when the channel translates them. They now follow the **Content language** in the preferences.
- **Interface language**: when you change the language in the preferences, KeyTune offers to restart at once and brings back the tabs of the session.
- **Win+D no longer sends KeyTune to the tray**: Win+D and Win+M while another program has focus only minimize KeyTune. It goes to the tray only when you minimize it while it has focus.
