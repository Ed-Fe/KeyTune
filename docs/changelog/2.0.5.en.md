## [2.0.5] - 2026-09-12

### Fixed

- **More reliable AutoDJ**: the music keeps playing even when the analysis is slow or fails; if needed, the next track starts with a regular transition.
- **More natural transitions**: AutoDJ identifies the rhythm of the songs better and avoids changes that seem too slow or too fast.
- **More stable volume**: the switch between tracks is better balanced, and the new song no longer stays quiet after the transition.
- **Original sound preserved**: bass and mids are no longer reduced during transitions; the AutoDJ profiles only adjust the volume handoff between songs.
- **YouTube Music playlists**: playlists with missing thumbnails keep loading, and playback can fall back to an alternative when a method fails.
- **Additional resources**: compatibility improvements make the installation and use of the AutoDJ and YouTube Music components more reliable.
