## [1.1.0] - 2026-06-29

### Added
- **Windows installer**: distributed as `KeyTune-Setup.exe`, with per-user or all-users installation, Start menu shortcuts, registration under *Default apps* and optional file extension association.
- **Automatic update**: downloads and installs without intervention, and is reflected in *Apps & features*.
- **Trending by country (YouTube Music)**: charts and highlights from more than 50 countries, organized by continent.
- **Moods and genres (YouTube Music)**: categories of moods and genres with related playlists.
- **Liked songs and History (YouTube Music)**: liked tracks and playback history, ready to play or add to playlists.
- **"About" screen**: version, license (MIT), links to the repository and library credits.
- **Getting started tutorial**: an interactive guide presenting features and settings.

### Changed
- **Single instance**: opening KeyTune again brings the window to the front. Files from Explorer play without stealing focus.
- **Smoother playback**: crossfade optimizations, lower resource use and fewer visual interruptions.
- **Faster interface**: rendering optimizations and better management of bindings.

### Fixed
- **Lasting YouTube Music connection**: the guide recommends exporting cookies in a private window.
- **Accessibility announcements**: fixed bugs with screen readers and focus in dialogs.
- **Bluetooth reconnection**: correct recovery after reconnecting.
- **Errors when switching tracks**: a rare bug that caused freezes.
