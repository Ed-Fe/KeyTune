## [2.1.0] - 2026-10-04

### Added

- **Folder explorer**: `Ctrl+E` opens, next to the tabs, a list with the folders and media files of the computer, starting from **This PC**.
  - It replaces the old folder browser, the folder mode that turned a tab into a list of files when you opened a folder with `Ctrl+Shift+O` or `Ctrl+Alt+O`.
  - `Enter` goes into the folder or plays the file.
  - `Shift+Enter` adds to the playlist without playing, and `Ctrl+Shift+F` puts it in the queue.
  - `Shift+F10` opens the menu with all the actions: play, add, open in a new playlist, convert, index in the library, copy, show in Windows Explorer and sort.
  - Selected folders go in with their subfolders.
  - The current folder and the sort order are remembered between sessions.

- **KeyTube, the YouTube hub**: KeyTube replaces the YouTube Music tab of versions up to 2.0.6 and also brings in regular YouTube.
  - Shortcut `Ctrl+Shift+Y`, or **View > KeyTube per tab**.
  - Connect your YouTube account in **Account and library**. The same account serves YouTube and YouTube Music.
  - Everything is in a single list, as in the folder explorer: `Enter` or `Right arrow` goes into the item and `Backspace` goes back.
  - **Home** shows your playlists and mixes, likes, history, videos from your subscriptions, subscribed channels, trending, and moods and genres.
  - Search has the **In** (YouTube Music or YouTube) and **Type** (songs, videos, albums, artists, channels and playlists) boxes.
  - A playlist, mix or video link pasted into the search field opens with `Enter`.
  - Inside a channel or artist, you choose what to see: videos, Shorts, live and playlists, or songs, albums, singles and similar artists.
  - Results come 20 at a time. Moving down past the last item loads the next ones, without taking focus away from its place.
  - **View details** (`Ctrl+Shift+I`) shows title, channel, duration, views, likes, date and description.
  - **View comments** (`Ctrl+Shift+M`) opens the comments in the list itself, with replies when YouTube.js is enabled.
  - On dubbed videos, **Playback > Audio language of the current media...** switches the dub on the spot, from the same point.
  - In **Preferences > KeyTube** and **Additional resources** you choose the content language and region and the default audio language.

- **YouTube live streams**: paste the link of a live stream into KeyTube and KeyTune plays it at the current moment.
  - The time bar shows a fixed live stream label. `T` tells you how long you have been watching.
  - Position jumps are refused with a notice.
  - If the connection drops, the player tries to reconnect up to three times and announces the loss and the recovery.
  - If the stream has already ended, KeyTune says so instead of playing the recording from the start.
  - **Show the video of live broadcasts**, in **Preferences > Playback**, works even with video output turned off. `Ctrl+Alt+V` toggles it during the broadcast.

- **Online radio**: `Ctrl+Shift+N`, or **View > Online radio per tab**, opens a tab with radio stations from all over the world, from the open Radio Browser directory.
  - The start page brings your favorites, the ones you listened to recently, the stations from your country, the most listened to in the world and the lists by country, genre and language.
  - Type a name in the search field, worldwide or only in your country, or paste the address of a stream to play it directly.
  - `Enter` plays the station and `Shift+Enter` adds it without playing. `Ctrl+D` favorites it.
  - The **Actions...** menu shows the details (country, language, genres, quality, votes), opens the website, copies the stream address and votes for the station.
  - The station plays as an audio-only live stream. The song the stream announces appears in the status bar and in the status announcement (`S`).

- **Add without playing and paste Windows files**: items go to the end of the playlist without interrupting what is playing.
  - `Ctrl+Shift+O` (**File > Open without playing...**) opens files without playing.
  - `Ctrl+Shift+V` (**Paste without playing**) pastes without playing.
  - `Ctrl+V` now accepts files and folders copied in Windows File Explorer, in addition to links and paths as text.

- **YouTube download**: `Ctrl+Shift+B`, or **File > Download from YouTube**, downloads songs and videos from YouTube and YouTube Music, in the background and through `yt-dlp`.
  - Choose audio or video, the quality, the sample rate and the folder.
  - If the chosen quality does not exist, the file is downloaded in the original one and the player says so.
  - A download never replaces a file that is already in the folder, and live streams are not downloaded.
  - With a playlist or album selected, **Download selection** and **Download whole playlist** download all the tracks, each playlist in a subfolder with its name. The limit is 200 items at a time.
  - During a download, `Ctrl+Shift+B` reports the progress and lets you cancel.
  - **Preferences > Download** keeps the default quality, folder and type, and the option to start right away without showing the dialog.
  - If FFmpeg is missing, KeyTune offers to download it from the official releases.

- **Media conversion**: `Ctrl+Shift+K`, or **File > Convert**, converts files on the computer.
  - Audio to video (a still image, with the album cover when there is one) and video to audio.
  - Audio to MP3, M4A, OGG, Opus, FLAC or WAV, and video to MP4, MKV, WebM, AVI or MOV.
  - KeyTune offers only the modes that suit the file type.
  - The converted file goes to the original's folder or to another one of your choice. The original is never changed.
  - The conversion runs in the background, with progress announced, and can be cancelled.
  - In a batch, KeyTune processes one item at a time and, at the end, offers the list of the ones that failed, with the reason.

- **One rule for converting and downloading**: `Ctrl+Shift+K` and `Ctrl+Shift+B` act on the selection when the focus is in a list (playlist, explorer or search results) and on the current media when the focus is on the player.

### Changed

- **Open and paste are now the same**: `Ctrl+O`, `Ctrl+V` and the explorer's `Enter` put the media in the current playlist and play it.
  - `Shift` always means "without playing".
  - Before, `Ctrl+O` replaced the playlist with the chosen files and `Ctrl+Shift+V` pasted into a new playlist. For a separate list, use `Ctrl+T` and open or paste into it.
  - A file from **File > Recent** also goes into the current playlist and plays.
  - In the YouTube Music search, `Enter` plays the selection and `Shift+Enter` adds it. **Open in new playlist** stays in the **Actions...** menu.
  - The "Open Media, Playlist or Folder" dialog (`Ctrl+Alt+O`) was removed. To open a link, copy it and use `Ctrl+V`.

- **Folders leave the tabs**: opening a folder no longer turns the tab into a folder browser; it shows up in the explorer. Old sessions with folder tabs reopen the last folder in the explorer.

- **Preferences in eight tabs, with groups**: **General**, **Playback**, **Accessibility**, **Library**, **Download**, **KeyTube**, **Online radio** and **Additional resources**.
  - Inside each tab the options are in groups, and the screen reader announces the group name when you enter it.
  - The help texts got shorter.

- **Shorter announcement when switching items**: when you switch track or tab, KeyTune says only the name and position ("Name. Item 3 of 10."), without the earlier "Current item:". It also no longer announces "Current folder: ..." in the explorer.

- **Less text read when opening dialogs**: repeated help is no longer read on entry. It is still in the tooltips.

- **Copying from the playlist**: `Ctrl+C` copies the selection as text and as files, so you can paste into another playlist, a text field or Windows Explorer. `Ctrl+Shift+C` always copies the path of the current media.

- **Playlist list without numbering**: items show only the name, without "1.", "2." at the start. The item that is playing is still marked with `▶`.

### Fixed

- **Closing a screen that is the only tab**: KeyTube, the equalizer and the other screens close and give way to an empty playlist, instead of refusing with "Cannot close the last tab".
- **Screen reader in groups and fields**: the equalizer, the preset editor, AutoDJ, KeyTube and the smart playlist editor now group their controls, and the number fields got names.
- **Window freezing when pasting**: large folders pasted with `Ctrl+V` are read in the background.
