## [1.0.0] - 2026-06-07

First official stable release of **KeyTune**. It brings together the main features for accessible media playback, tabbed playlists, equalization, an advanced diagnostics system and full YouTube Music integration.

### Feature summary

- **Accessible interface**: fully optimized for screen readers, with robust shortcuts and audible announcements.
- **Tabbed playlists**: multiple playlists in tabs, track reordering, and saving and loading `.m3u` and `.m3u8` files.
- **Folder browser**: Explorer style, with automatic preview.
- **Session restore**: picks up where you left off — tabs, track, position, volume and settings.
- **Equalizer per tab**: independent sound for each playlist, 18 presets, customize or apply to all tabs.
- **YouTube Music integration**: search, and open mixes and playlists. Import browser cookies; audio resolution is automatic.
- **File association**: register and unregister extensions to open them with KeyTune.
- **Diagnostic logs**: detailed reports with a configurable level of detail.
- **Automatic update**: detects new versions, shows what is new and installs automatically.

### Recent adjustments and improvements
- **Consistent shortcuts**: `Ctrl+W` closes a tab, `Ctrl+Shift+W` stops playback.
- **Multiple selection**: `Ctrl+Arrow` and `Ctrl+Space` in YouTube Music results.
- **Better navigation**: fixed focus with Tab.
- **YouTube Music stability**: improved recovery, authentication and likes.
