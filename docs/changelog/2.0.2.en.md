## [2.0.2] - 2026-09-05

### Fixed

- **Features missing in version 2.0.0**: this update delivers the YouTube.js integration and the installation of additional resources, announced in the 2.0.0 release notes but not included in the published package.
- **Additional resources**: the YouTube libraries, YouTube.js, portable Node.js and the AutoDJ analyzer are now distributed separately from the main installer. In **Preferences > Additional resources**, the components needed are shown before downloading and have their integrity verified.
- **Playback with YouTube.js**: the player now offers media resolution through YouTube.js, sharing Node.js with yt-dlp and falling back to yt-dlp when needed.
- **AutoDJ and playback**: includes fixes for playback continuity and for transitions between tracks.
- **Plugins and documentation**: includes adjustments to the marketplace and installation dialogs, plus the plugin guides in Portuguese, English and Spanish for offline reference.
