## [1.2.0] - 2026-07-05

### Added
- **Translation system (localization)**: the interface can now be translated into other languages. Portuguese (Brazil) is the source language, and English and Spanish are already included. Choose the language in *Settings > Preferences > General > Language* (or leave it on Automatic to follow the system). The manual, the credits and the installer can also be translated. Translators: see `docs/translations.md`.
- **Playback queue**: adds an independent queue to organize what plays next, with actions to add, manage, remove, reorder and clear items.
- **Lyrics panel**: finds and shows lyrics automatically when the track changes, with a quick toggle, keyboard reading and copying of the content.
- **Playback speed and pitch**: adds shortcuts to increase, decrease and reset the speed (`]`, `[`, `\`) and to change or reset the pitch in semitones (`Shift+]`, `Shift+[`, `Shift+\`).
