## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **YouTube notifications**: the **Notifications** item, in KeyTube's home (or **View > YouTube notifications**), shows your account's notification inbox. `Enter` plays the video and marks the notification as read, and the **Actions...** menu has **Mark as read** and **Hide this notification**. KeyTune also speaks new notifications: it checks the inbox every 15 minutes, and the interval is in **Preferences > KeyTube > Announce new notifications**, where you can turn it off.
- **Reply to and rate comments**: with the account connected, the comments list starts with the **Write a comment...** line, which posts a new comment, and a comment's **Actions...** menu has **Reply...**, **Like the comment** and **Dislike the comment**. On your own comments, **Edit my comment...** and **Delete my comment...** appear. What you write is published on YouTube under your name.
- **Channel subscription and bell**: **Channel subscription and notifications...**, one of the lines inside a YouTube channel and also in its **Actions...** menu, subscribes, unsubscribes and chooses what the channel notifies you of: everything, personalized or nothing. **About the channel** opens the channel's description, numbers and links in a reading box.
- **Channel posts**: inside a YouTube channel, **Posts** lists what it published in its posts tab. `Enter` reads the whole post and `Right arrow` opens its comments. Images are not shown.
- **YouTube history**: the regular YouTube videos you watch go into the account's history, as YouTube Music tracks already did, and KeyTube's home gained the **YouTube history** list. It works with YouTube.js turned on.
- **Live chat**: **View the live chat**, in the **Actions...** menu of a broadcast on air (or **Playback > View live chat of the current media**), opens the chat in the list. New messages come in by themselves and are spoken, and **Send a message...** sends one with your account. **Stop speaking the chat messages** turns the speech off.
- **Search in this channel**: inside a YouTube channel, **Search in this channel...** lists its videos that match the text.
- **Newest comments**: **Sort by newest**, in the **Actions...** menu of a comments list, swaps the top comments for the newest ones.
- **Vote in polls**: with the account connected, a post with a poll has **Vote in the poll...**.
- **Windows notification**: with KeyTune minimized, in the tray or behind another window, the notice of a new YouTube notification goes to the Windows notification center, and activating it opens the list. You can turn it off in **Preferences > KeyTube**.
- **Load the whole list**: in the **Actions...** menu of the YouTube tab, brings what is missing from the list all at once, without going down to the end several times. In a playlist, every track comes. In a search or a channel, which have no known end, up to 1000 items come at a time.
- **Alert sounds**: short sounds tell you what happens without relying on speech. In the live chat: a new message, a paid message, a message that mentions your name, a message sent and the chat ending. On the YouTube account: a new notification, liking, removing the like or disliking, and an action that worked or failed (commenting, subscribing, voting). In the player: the end or the start of a list, a folder or list opened, going back, more items loading, KeyTune opened or closed, the welcome screen, a new update, a tab opened or closed, an item put in the queue or taken out of it, a finished download, the sleep timer warning and a confirmation question. They play apart from the media volume. In **Preferences > Accessibility** you can turn all of them off, set their volume and, in the **Sounds that play** list, turn them off one by one or replace the sound of an event with an audio file from your computer.

### Changed

- **History in two lists**: the **History** item, in KeyTube's home, is now called **YouTube Music history**, next to the new **YouTube history**. The **Save what I listened to in the YouTube Music history** option became **Save what I listened to and watched in the YouTube history** and now applies to videos too.
- **KeyTube actions menu**: the menu of the **Actions...** button (`Shift+F10`) shows only what applies to the selected item, in blocks: what `Enter` and the arrows do, what else you can do with the item, where to take the selection and, last, what applies to the list. Before, it always had the same items, with the ones that did not apply disabled. **View contents** is now called **Open** on folders, channels and artists, and **View replies** on a comment.
- **Items loaded at a time**: the **Playlists loaded at a time** option, in **Preferences > KeyTube**, was renamed and now applies to every list of the tab: library playlists, search results, playlist tracks and comments. Before, those other lists always came 20 at a time.
- **Long playlists load faster**: when going down a YouTube Music playlist, KeyTune reuses the tracks it had already fetched. Before, each new part asked for the whole playlist again.
- **YouTube playlist without a limit**: opening or downloading a regular YouTube playlist brings every video. Before, it stopped at 200.

### Fixed

- **YouTube.js plays audio only**: playback through YouTube.js now asks YouTube for just the track's audio, which is much smaller than the 360p video used until now, and only falls back to the video if the audio doesn't come through.
- **Complete liked songs**: the **Liked songs** list on the YouTube Music home shows every track the account has liked. Before, it stopped at the first few hundred, while the same playlist opened from **Your playlists and mixes** came in full. It now loads a part at a time: go down to the end of the list to bring more.
- **YouTube titles in your language**: the video lists of YouTube channels and playlists always came with English titles when the channel translates them. They now follow the **Content language** in the preferences. And with that language set to **Same as KeyTune**, changing the interface language applies to the content at once, without reopening the player.
- **Interface language without reopening by hand**: when you change the interface language in the preferences, KeyTune offers to restart at once. It closes, opens again in the chosen language and brings back the tabs of the session.
- **Music no longer stops because of another YouTube action**: YouTube.js now runs in two processes, one only for playing and one for everything else (comments, notifications, chat, channels). Before, an action that took too long to answer brought down the single process and cut the track that was playing. Now it fails on its own and the music goes on.
- **No repeated comments or messages**: if the connection to YouTube.js dropped right after you commented, replied, voted or sent a chat message, KeyTune sent the action again and it could go out twice. Now only lookups are repeated; an action that changes something in your account never is.
- **Live chat at YouTube's pace**: KeyTune now waits the interval YouTube asks for between requests (at least 4 seconds) instead of always asking at the same rhythm. And a message that YouTube blocks until the account is verified is now reported as not sent, instead of looking sent.
- **Notifications kept apart per account**: when you switch YouTube accounts, KeyTune no longer mixes the notifications already announced for one with the other's.
