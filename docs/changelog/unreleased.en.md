## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **M4A downloads**: **Audio quality**, in Preferences and in the download dialog, has a new **M4A (AAC)** option. On YouTube, KeyTune takes the audio that already comes in M4A, without conversion and without needing FFmpeg, at a quality slightly below Original. From a site without M4A, the audio is converted with FFmpeg; without FFmpeg, it comes in the site's format, and the end-of-download notice says which.
- **User manual in a program window**: **Help > Open user manual** (and the button on the welcome screen) no longer opens the browser. The window has five fields: **Search the manual**, **Sections**, **Topics in the section**, **Text** and **Links in the topic**. Choose with the arrow keys and press `Enter` to go to the next field; `Esc` closes. Each field also has a shortcut: `Alt+S` goes to the search, `Alt+T` to the sections, `Alt+O` to the topics, `Alt+X` to the text and `Alt+L` to the links. The manual's links work: `Enter` on an item of **Links in the topic**, or on a line of the text that has a link, follows the link. A part of the manual opens in the window itself, with the focus on the text; a website opens in the browser. The window does not block the player: you can keep listening and using the shortcuts while it is open, and `Alt+F6` switches between the manual and the main window. Opening the manual again brings back the window that is already open. In the search, type one or more words and press `Enter`: the lists show only the topics that have all of them (ignoring case and accents), the program says how many it found and the text opens on the line with the word. `Enter` with the field empty brings the whole manual back. Each topic is short, and the shortcut tables become "key: what it does" lines. It follows the program language.
- **Spoken notices split by kind**: in **Preferences > Accessibility**, the **Notices that speak** list now has eight new kinds, besides the five event kinds that already existed: playback; track change and SponsorBlock; speed and pitch; modes turned on or off; navigation; progress; confirmations; and errors and unavailable actions. You can silence the track change and keep the time, volume and status you ask for with `T`, `V` and `S`, for example. Those three keys do not depend on the list. All of them come on, and the **Enable accessibility announcements** switch still applies on top. The option to speak while KeyTune is active still covers only the notices that arrive by themselves (chat, notifications, download and sleep timer).
- **Open KeyTube on startup**: in **Preferences > General > Startup and session**, **Open KeyTube on startup** opens the KeyTube tab together with the app. It is off by default and available only with the YouTube integration turned on in **Additional resources**.

### Changed

- **`Alt`+letter shortcuts on dialog fields**: the fields on every Preferences tab and in the download, conversion, change history, update, playback queue, audio output, equalizer preset, plugin, YouTube connection and quick shortcuts help dialogs now have an underlined letter in their label. `Alt` plus that letter moves the focus straight to the field. Letters that were repeated within the same dialog changed, so each shortcut leads to one place only. In the download dialog, the **Download** list is now called **What to download**.
