## [Unreleased]

The main news in this version: KeyTune now follows your YouTube account from the inside. You read and reply to comments, receive notifications, join live chats, subscribe to channels, read posts and vote in polls, all from the keyboard and with a screen reader. YouTube videos also get what music already had: history, resuming where you stopped (on your phone and TV too), radio and related videos, chapters and transcript. The KeyTube home was reorganized into **YouTube** and **YouTube Music**, and short sounds tell you what happens without relying on speech. The details are below.

### Added

- **YouTube notifications**: **Notifications** shows the account's notification inbox. `Enter` plays the video and marks the notification as read, and the **Actions...** menu has **Mark as read** and **Hide this notification**. KeyTune checks the inbox every 15 minutes and speaks the new notifications. With KeyTune minimized, in the tray or behind another window, the notice goes to the Windows notification center, and activating it opens the list. The interval and that notice are in **Preferences > KeyTube**, where you can turn both off.
- **Comments**: with the account connected, the comments list starts with **Write a comment...**. A comment's **Actions...** menu has **Reply...**, **Like the comment** and **Dislike the comment**, and your own comments also show **Edit my comment...** and **Delete my comment...**. What you write is published on YouTube under your name. **Sort by newest**, in the list's **Actions...** menu, swaps the top comments for the newest ones.
- **Live chat**: **View the live chat**, in the **Actions...** menu of a broadcast on air (or **Playback > View live chat of the current media**), opens the chat in the list. New messages come in by themselves and are spoken, and **Stop speaking the chat messages** turns the speech off. **Send a message...** is the last line of the list, after the newest messages, and the list opens on it. The message goes out with your account.
- **Channels**: inside a YouTube channel, **Channel subscription and notifications...** (also in the channel's **Actions...** menu) subscribes, unsubscribes and chooses what the channel notifies you of: everything, personalized or nothing. **About the channel** opens the description, numbers and links in a reading box. **Search in this channel...** lists its videos that match the text. **Posts** lists what the channel published: `Enter` reads the whole post, `Right arrow` opens its comments and, with the account connected, a post with a poll has **Vote in the poll...**. Images in posts are not shown.
- **Shorts**: **Shorts** opens a tab that plays one short at a time, repeating until you move on to the next one, as on a phone.
- **Watch later and liked videos**: in a video's **Actions...** menu, **Save to Watch later** keeps the video in the account's playlist, and **Remove from Watch later** removes it while you are in it. **Liked YouTube videos** lists the videos the account liked.
- **YouTube history**: the regular YouTube videos you watch go into the account's history, as YouTube Music tracks already did. It works with YouTube.js turned on and with **Save what I listened to and watched in the YouTube history** on.
- **Resume where you stopped**: long YouTube videos come back from the point where you stopped, like podcasts, and **Continue listening** lists them. When you press `Enter` on a video in **YouTube history** that you left halfway, KeyTune starts where the account left off and announces the position. While the video plays, KeyTune tells YouTube how far you watched, every half minute and when the video leaves, so your phone, your TV and the site show the video at the same point. It needs the account connected and YouTube.js turned on.
- **Chapters and transcript**: in the **Actions...** menu of a YouTube video (and in **Playback**, for the one that is playing), **View chapters** lists the chapters and plays the video from the chosen point, and **View transcript** shows the subtitles as text, with the time of each line.
- **Load the whole list**: in the **Actions...** menu of the YouTube tab, brings what is missing from the list all at once, without going down to the end several times. In a playlist, every track comes. In a search or a channel, which have no known end, up to 1000 items come at a time.
- **KeyTube home in groups**: the home has four items. **YouTube** and **YouTube Music** each hold what belongs to their service, while **Trending** and **Moods and genres** stay on their own. **YouTube** has **Recommended on YouTube** (what YouTube suggests for the account), **Watch later**, **Liked YouTube videos**, **YouTube history**, **Shorts** and **Notifications**, besides the videos from subscriptions and the subscribed channels. **YouTube Music** has **For you on YouTube Music**, with the rows of the YouTube Music home, each one as a folder.
- **Alert sounds**: short sounds tell you what happens without relying on speech, and play apart from the media volume. In the live chat: a new message, a paid message, a message that mentions your name, a message sent and the chat ending. On the YouTube account: a new notification, liking, removing the like or disliking, and an action that worked or failed (commenting, subscribing, voting). In the player: the end or the start of a list, a folder or list opened, going back, more items loading, KeyTune opened or closed, the welcome screen, a new update, a tab opened or closed, an item put in the queue or taken out of it, a finished download, the sleep timer warning and a confirmation question. In **Preferences > Accessibility** you can turn all of them off, set their volume and, in the **Sounds that play** list, turn them off one by one or replace the sound of an event with an audio file from your computer.

### Changed

- **Radio and related videos for YouTube videos**: **Start radio from this track** (`Ctrl+R`) and related autoplay, when the video is a regular YouTube video, use the videos YouTube itself suggests next. Before, they used the YouTube Music radio, which only made sense for songs.
- **Announcements that do not interrupt the screen reader**: KeyTune announcements wait for the reader to finish what it is saying instead of cutting it off. In **Preferences > Accessibility**, **Interrupt the screen reader speech** brings the old behavior back, **Speak event notices while KeyTune is active** chooses whether the notices that arrive by themselves speak in the window, and the **Event notices that speak** list turns each one on or off: chat messages, message sent or chat ended, YouTube notifications, finished download and sleep timer.
- **History in two lists**: **History** is now called **YouTube Music history**, next to the **YouTube history**. The **Save what I listened to in the YouTube Music history** option became **Save what I listened to and watched in the YouTube history** and now applies to videos too.
- **KeyTube actions menu**: the menu of the **Actions...** button (`Shift+F10`) shows only what applies to the selected item, in blocks: what `Enter` and the arrows do, what else you can do with the item, where to take the selection and, last, what applies to the list. Before, it always had the same items, with the ones that did not apply disabled. **View contents** is now called **Open** on folders, channels and artists, and **View replies** on a comment.
- **Items loaded at a time**: the **Playlists loaded at a time** option, in **Preferences > KeyTube**, was renamed and now applies to every list of the tab: library playlists, search results, playlist tracks and comments. Before, those other lists always came 20 at a time.
- **Long playlists load faster**: when going down a YouTube Music playlist, KeyTune reuses the tracks it had already fetched. Before, each new part asked for the whole playlist again.
- **YouTube playlist without a limit**: opening or downloading a regular YouTube playlist brings every video. Before, it stopped at 200.

### Fixed

- **AutoDJ really off**: with AutoDJ turned off, a saved session no longer analyzes or mixes tracks, even with crossfade on, and **Play playlist with AutoDJ** is only available when it is on. Analysis also stopped failing when Windows still held the analyzer's temporary file.
- **KeyTune not reopening after closing or updating**: if shutdown hung, KeyTune stayed alive in the background with no window, and opening it again showed nothing. Shutdown now always finishes, and launching KeyTune ends a stalled copy and opens normally. If an update does not finish, KeyTune says so the next time it opens.
- **Complete liked songs**: the **Liked songs** list of YouTube Music shows every track the account has liked. Before, it stopped at the first few hundred, while the same playlist opened from **Your playlists and mixes** came in full. It now loads a part at a time: go down to the end of the list to bring more.
- **YouTube titles in your language**: the video lists of YouTube channels and playlists always came with English titles when the channel translates them. They now follow the **Content language** in the preferences. And with that language set to **Same as KeyTune**, changing the interface language applies to the content at once, without reopening the player.
- **Interface language without reopening by hand**: when you change the interface language in the preferences, KeyTune offers to restart at once. It closes, opens again in the chosen language and brings back the tabs of the session.
- **Win+D no longer sends KeyTune to the tray**: with **When minimizing, hide in the system tray** on, Win+D (and Win+M) while another program has focus now only minimizes KeyTune, and Win+D again brings it back. It goes to the tray only when you minimize it while it has focus.
