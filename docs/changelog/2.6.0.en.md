## [2.6.0] - 2026-11-27

This release adds a portable version, YouTube links that open straight in KeyTune, the manual in a program window and a monitoring output, alongside fixes in KeyTube, saved playlists and AutoDJ.

### Added

- **Portable version**: extract `KeyTune-Portable.zip` and open `KeyTune.exe`. Data stays in the `data` folder, and each portable folder opens in its own instance, with file associations separate from the installed version. If the folder is read-only, KeyTune warns that it will write to this computer.
- **YouTube links open in KeyTune** (Windows): a YouTube or YouTube Music video or playlist link clicked in WhatsApp, email or another program plays straight in the player. Since Windows does not let a program take only one site's links, KeyTune registers as a browser and sends the other links to the browser you used, or to the one you choose in **Browser for other links**. To turn it on, use **Make KeyTune the default browser...** in **Preferences > General > YouTube links** (or the **Offer KeyTune as a browser** option in the installer). To undo it, choose another default browser in Windows.
- **Manual in a program window**: **Help > Open user manual** opens the manual in a KeyTune window instead of the browser, with the contents on the left and the text on the right, like Windows help. The search shows only the topics with all the words you typed, links work, and the window does not block the player: `Alt+F6` switches between it and the main window.
- **Monitoring output** (Windows): in **Preferences > Playback**, you can send a copy of the audio to headphones or speakers on another device. The arrow keys adjust the headphone level, and the output feeding RadioBOSS stays at the **Broadcast volume** level.
- **M4A downloads**: **Audio quality**, in Preferences and in the download dialog, has a new **M4A (AAC)** option. On YouTube the audio already comes in that format, so there is no conversion and no need for FFmpeg, at a quality slightly below the original. On other sites the audio is converted with FFmpeg; without it, it comes in the site's format, and the end-of-download notice says which.
- **Add to the playlist from Explorer**: the File Explorer context menu offers **Add to KeyTune playlist** for associated formats, even when another player is the default. The files go to the end of the playlist without interrupting playback. The option is registered during installation or in **Preferences > General**.
- **Spoken notices by kind**: in **Preferences > Accessibility**, the **Notices that speak** list has eight kinds besides the five event kinds, such as track change, navigation, progress and errors. You can silence the track change, for example, and still hear the time, volume and status when you ask with `T`, `V` and `S`. All of them come on.
- **Open KeyTube on startup**: the option in **Preferences > General > Startup and session** opens the KeyTube tab together with the app. It is off by default and only shows up with the YouTube integration turned on.

### Changed

- **`Alt`+letter shortcuts in dialogs**: the fields in Preferences and in the download, conversion, change history, update, queue, audio output, equalizer, plugin, YouTube connection and shortcut help dialogs have an underlined letter in their label, and `Alt` plus that letter moves the focus straight to the field. In the download dialog, the **Download** list is now called **What to download**.
- **Opened files play outside the playlist**: `Enter` on a file in the folder explorer, and a file opened from Windows File Explorer, went into the current playlist. They now play in the **Outside the playlist** tab, replaced on each file, and the playlist stays as it was. To build a playlist, use `Shift+Enter` in the explorer or **Add to KeyTune playlist** in Windows. To go back to the old way, uncheck **Play opened files without adding them to the playlist** in **Preferences > Playback**.

### Fixed

- **Alert sounds in the background**: with the window minimized, in the tray or unfocused, a new YouTube notification played the Windows sound instead of KeyTune's. Changing tracks while KeyTube was still loading also gave the Windows beep.
- **First item of a new page**: reaching the end of a search or radio list in KeyTube with Down Arrow and loading the next page left the first new item unannounced by the screen reader.
- **Track change blocked**: next or previous in a favorite radio station, or in playlists that aren't YouTube Music, was blocked while YouTube Music was doing another operation, such as loading the library.
- **Related content**: moving into similar tracks at the end of a YouTube Music playlist only announced "Related content", without the track name or position.
- **Shorts that didn't open**: now and then Shorts showed "YouTube returned no shorts right now". They now open like the site's Shorts tab.
- **Names in saved playlists**: reopening a saved playlist with YouTube videos and songs showed the tracks as `watch?v=...`. Playlists saved before only get their names back once saved again.
- **yt-dlp downloaded for no reason**: the additional resources check downloaded yt-dlp every time, even without a new version.
- **AutoDJ with large playlists**: with hundreds of songs, AutoDJ analysis could leave the whole computer, screen reader included, unresponsive for minutes.
- **Starting an AutoDJ session**: **Play playlist with AutoDJ** was disabled or only said it was unavailable. The command now says what is keeping the session from starting: AutoDJ turned off, the additional resources not installed, or the playlist empty or loading.
