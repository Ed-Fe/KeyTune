## [2.3.1] - 2026-10-10

### Changed

- **Download from other sites**: `Ctrl+Shift+B` now also downloads addresses from other sites that KeyTune plays and direct links to audio or video files. Before, only YouTube and YouTube Music media were accepted. Radios and live broadcasts are still left out, because they never end, and from a list address only the first item is downloaded.
- **Download queue without a limit**: **Download selection** and **Download whole playlist** download every item. Before, the queue stopped at 200 and the rest was left out. KeyTune still asks for confirmation with the number of items before starting.
- **Faster download queue**: for a selection or a playlist, KeyTune downloads three items at the same time. Before, it downloaded one at a time. The status bar shows the position in the queue, and no longer the percentage of each item.

### Fixed

- **Connecting the YouTube account right after opening KeyTune**: connecting with a `cookies.txt` or with pasted cookies failed with "does not contain a compatible YouTube Music authentication cookie", even with a correct export. It happened to anyone who connected the account before using anything from YouTube, such as right after installing. The account now connects in that situation too.
- **Cookies without `LOGIN_INFO`**: anyone who copied the cookies from the browser console, or used an exporter that leaves out protected cookies, was told they had expired. The message now says `LOGIN_INFO` is missing and asks you to export all youtube.com cookies to a `cookies.txt`. If the account connects even without it, KeyTune warns that tracks that require the account may not play.
